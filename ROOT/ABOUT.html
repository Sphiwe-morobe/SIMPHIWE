About Us

Discover the story behind our artisan bakery.

Our Story

The Daily Loaf Bakery was established in 2012 by the Johnson family. What began as a small weekend stall at a local farmer's market, known for our sourdough bread and croissants, quickly grew in popularity.

Due to overwhelming demand, we opened our retail storefront in the heart of Cape Town's vibrant city centre. Today, we remain a cornerstone of the local community, celebrated for our commitment to using organic, locally-sourced ingredients and traditional baking methods.

Our Mission

To craft exceptional, hand-made baked goods using the finest local ingredients, fostering a sense of community and joy with every loaf.

Our Vision

To be the most beloved and sustainable artisan bakery in the region, known not just for our products, but also for our positive impact on local producers and our community.
